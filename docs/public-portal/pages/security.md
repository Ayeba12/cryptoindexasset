## Page copy

### S01 · Hero

H1: Take care with account access and asset details

Keep your sign-in information private and check transaction instructions carefully. If something about your account looks wrong, contact support with the details you can safely share.

Button: Contact support → `/contact`

### S02 · Account access

H2: Protect your sign-in details

Use a unique password for your account. Keep your email account protected too, especially if you use it for password recovery.

Do not include passwords, recovery phrases, private keys or one-time security codes in a support message. If you receive unexpected instructions to send money, check the request through the contact details on this website before acting.

### S03 · Asset custody

H2: Understand who holds the assets

Before you fund an account, ask who holds the assets, what rights you have and how withdrawal requests work. Account access controls and asset custody answer different questions.

Links: Read the risk disclosure → `/risk-disclosure` · Read the terms → `/terms`

### S04 · Account concern

H2: Tell us what happened

Include the approximate time, the activity you noticed and any relevant transaction reference. Share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Button: Report an account concern → `/contact`

Link: Read account FAQs → `/faq`
