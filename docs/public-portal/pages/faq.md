### Q02 · Accounts

H2: Accounts

H3: How do I create an account?

Open the registration page, enter your details and follow the setup instructions. Before funding the account, read the service terms, applicable fees and deposit instructions.

Link: Create account → `/register`

H3: How do I sign in?

Use the email address and password for your account. If you cannot remember your password, use the recovery link on the sign-in page.

Link: Sign in → `/login`

H3: Is the service available where I live?

Availability depends on the service's eligibility requirements. Check the current terms or contact us before registering or sending funds.

Link: Read the terms → `/terms`

### Q03 · Investment and fees

H2: Investment and fees

H3: What is copy trading?

Copy trading follows another trader's transactions under a service's rules. Your results may differ from the trader's, and losses are possible. Review the execution process, costs and conditions before deciding to participate.

Link: Read about copy trading → `/copy-trading`

H3: Are returns guaranteed?

No. Crypto assets can lose value, including the full amount invested. A previous return or a high win rate does not guarantee a future result.

Link: Read the risk disclosure → `/risk-disclosure`

H3: What fees will I pay?

Check the charges for the service and activity you are considering. Ask for the current schedule if a fee or calculation is unclear. Missing fee information does not mean a service is free.

Link: Review fees → `/fees`

H3: What is the minimum investment?

Check the terms of the specific option, including the currency used. Contact us for current details if the minimum amount is not shown.

Link: Read the investor guide → `/investors`

### Q04 · Funding and withdrawals

H2: Funding and withdrawals

H3: Which asset and network should I use for a deposit?

Use the current instructions in your account. Check both the asset and network before sending funds. Contact support if the instructions are missing or unclear.

H3: How do I request a withdrawal?

Sign in, open the withdrawal area and check the conditions before entering your details. Review the destination and amount before sending the request.

H3: Does approval mean a transfer is complete?

Approval means the request has passed review. Wait for separate transfer confirmation and any available transaction reference before treating it as completed.

H3: How long will a withdrawal take?

Check the current processing information for your request. Review and transfer timing can differ. Contact support if you need an update; do not assume that a submitted request settles immediately.

Link: See how it works → `/how-it-works`
