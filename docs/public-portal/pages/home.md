## Layout

Desktop order: shared header → H01 centred hero with wide product image below → H02 asymmetric account bento → H03 process strip → H04 copy-trading introduction → H05 decision links → H06 questions → H07 closing action → shared footer.

Follow the [public portal visual direction](../visual-direction.md) for the responsive type scale and spacing. Centre the hero copy and actions, then show a wide account illustration beneath the risk line. Keep the product image prominent, with a separate narrow composition for mobile. Begin with a static illustration of balance and activity regions, without invented money or gains. Caption it "Illustrative account view". Avoid fake proof counters beneath the hero.

H02 uses a 7:5 bento composition on wide screens. The larger balances panel sits beside a smaller request-status panel with the account-process link below it. Preserve the two existing H3 topics; do not invent another feature to fill a card. H03 is an open numbered sequence. H04 has one explanation and one link beside a clearly labelled conceptual visual, not a trader carousel. H05 uses open link rows for its three distinct destinations. H06 contains visible short answers. Only H01 and H07 are centred.

Mobile: copy, hero actions, risk line, illustration, then sections in the same order. Stack process steps vertically. Keep each action with the text it follows. No floating registration bar obscuring copy.

H2: Understand the strategy behind the trader

Copy trading follows another trader's decisions. Before choosing a service, look at how trades are copied, what the trader holds and which costs apply. A strong past result is only part of the picture.

No. Requests are reviewed. Check the withdrawal conditions and any applicable costs before you submit one.
