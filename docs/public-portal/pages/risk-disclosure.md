## Page copy

### R01 · Introduction

H1: Understand the risks before you invest

Crypto assets can lose value. You could lose all the money you invest. Read the terms of the service and ask about anything you do not understand before sending funds.

### R02 · Market risk

H2: Prices can change sharply

An asset's value may fall while you hold it. It may also be difficult to sell or transfer an asset when you want to. An account balance does not guarantee what you can receive from a sale.

### R03 · Copy-trading risk

H2: Copying a trader can produce different results

Past performance does not guarantee future results. Timing and execution differences can affect copied transactions. Read how the particular service operates and how costs affect your outcome.

### R04 · Custody and access

H2: Understand how assets are held

Your ability to recover assets can depend on the provider's arrangements and financial position. Review the custody and ownership terms before funding an account.

### R05 · Withdrawals

H2: Access to funds can be restricted

Read the withdrawal conditions, including review steps and any restrictions. Submitting or approving a request does not, by itself, confirm that a transfer has settled.

### R06 · Operational risks

H2: Services and transactions can be interrupted

Technical failures or a service interruption may affect access to your account or the processing of activity. Check transaction details carefully and keep relevant records.

### R07 · Costs and suitability

H2: Review the full terms

Costs affect the amount you keep. Check the fees, eligibility conditions and any obligations relevant to your circumstances. Seek qualified advice if you need help assessing them.
