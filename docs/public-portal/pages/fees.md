### F01 · Hero

H1: Understand the cost before you commit

Review the charges that apply to your activity and how they are calculated. Check the full cost of funding, participating in a service and withdrawing.

### F02 · Schedule fallback

H2: Current fee information

A fee schedule is not currently available on this page. Ask us for the charges that apply to the service you are considering before you proceed.

Button: Ask about fees → `/contact`

### F03 · Cost explanations

H2: What to check

H3: Platform and service charges

Ask whether there is an account fee, service charge or commission. Confirm whether a charge is fixed, percentage-based or linked to a result.

H3: Trading and conversion costs

Check whether a transaction includes trading charges, a difference between buying and selling prices, or a currency conversion cost.

H3: Funding and withdrawal costs

Confirm any platform charge and any separate network or payment-provider cost. Check which currency each amount uses.

### F04 · Worked examples, conditional

H2: See how a charge is calculated

Use the confirmed fee rule to check the calculation before agreeing to a transaction.

### F05 · Enquiry

H2: Need a cost explained?

Tell us which service or transaction you are considering. Ask for the amount, calculation and any conditions that apply.

## Publication dependencies

D03 applies. This is deliberately not a fabricated pricing schedule. The business must supply actual fee categories, rates, currencies, minimums, taxes if applicable, effective date and change policy. F04 examples must show inputs, the actual rule and the result, including a non-profitable case if a performance fee exists. Remove F04 entirely until those facts are confirmed.
