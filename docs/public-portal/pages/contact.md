# Contact

Route: `/contact` | Purpose: collect useful enquiries and help existing users explain problems.

Primary action: Send message, to a confirmed support endpoint.

SEO title: Contact Crypto Index Asset

Meta description: Ask Crypto Index Asset about the service, fees or an account issue. Tell us what you need help with and include a relevant reference.

Primary topic: contact Crypto Index Asset. Search intent: support and pre-sales.

## Layout

Header → T01 hero → T02 form with T03 contact guidance alongside → T04 related links → footer.

Desktop: form takes the wider column, guidance the narrower column. Mobile: brief guidance first, then form, then alternative contact. Fields use visible labels. Topic selection comes before Message to help people frame the enquiry. No map until a real public business location is confirmed.

After submission, replace the form only on confirmed delivery. Preserve ordinary text after a recoverable failure. Do not copy the contents into analytics or URLs.
