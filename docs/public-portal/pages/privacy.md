## Page copy

### P01 · Introduction

H1: Privacy policy

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit Crypto Index Asset, create an account or contact support. It also explains how to ask questions about your information.

Internal fields: [DATA_CONTROLLER], [ADDRESS], [PRIVACY_CONTACT], [EFFECTIVE_DATE], [VERSION].

### P02 · Information

H2: Information associated with your account

Account information can include the name and email address you provide, sign-in information and records of account activity. A support enquiry can include your contact details, message and any reference you supply.

Internal inventory to confirm against the deployed service:

| Category | Observed repository fields | Facts needed for policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email, phone, password hash | Required fields, collection purpose and lawful basis where applicable |
| Financial activity | Asset balances, withdrawal amount, wallet or bank destination | Exact fields used, recipients and retention |
| Authentication | Session cookies and login records | Cookie names, duration, processing and access |
| Enquiries | Proposed name, email, topic and message | Support provider, storage and deletion process |
| Website usage | Not established by this review | Analytics, tracking, IP/device data and consent controls if applicable |

### P03 · Use and sharing

H2: How information is used

Internal clause to complete: state the actual purpose of each category, including account operation, authentication, requests, support and any legal obligations. Identify the applicable lawful basis where required. Do not introduce optional marketing as a condition of account access.

H2: Service providers and other recipients

Internal clause to complete: identify the actual hosting, database, authentication, email, payment and support recipients, what they receive, why, and any international transfers. A package dependency alone is not proof that a provider receives production data.

H2: Cookies and similar technology

Internal clause to complete: list actual cookies, purpose and duration. Distinguish necessary account access from optional analytics or marketing. Describe working controls, if any.

### P04 · Retention and requests

Contact us to ask how your information is handled or to raise a privacy concern. Describe your request without including your password or other secret credentials.
