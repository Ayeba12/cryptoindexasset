## Research notes

Search intent was checked with a small public search sample for copy trading and crypto investment risks. Results commonly use explainers, trader information, fee details and risk notices. This supports an explainer and decision journey, not a projected-return landing page. This is an editorial inference, not a measured keyword or competitor ranking study. Search volumes, ranking difficulty and conversion performance are unknown.

General risk wording was checked against [Investor.gov's crypto asset investor alert](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/crypto-asset-securities) and [FINRA's crypto asset risk overview](https://www.finra.org/investors/investing/investment-products/crypto-assets/risks). These explain potential losses, volatility and platform risks. They do not establish the law governing this business or its regulatory status.

[eToro's copy trading risk notice](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/risk-warnings/) was used as a primary industry example for performance and execution caveats. It is not evidence about Crypto Index Asset. No competitor fees, eligibility rules, product names or protection claims were transferred into this copy.

## Editorial self-check

Titles, descriptions, H1s, internal destinations, section order and state copy are supplied. Keywords are editorial targets, not measured opportunities. No precise figure was added merely to meet a generic writing checklist. No personal investment recommendation is made.

Long-form policy sections remain dependent on business facts. Content-writer's narrative registry and shared contract files are not included in the installed standalone skill. No registry events, canon IDs or approval receipts were invented. The handoff manifest records null values for those dependencies and a hash for each page.

Verve and unslop were applied to the new draft, rather than used to preserve unsupported claims in the legacy text. UX writing checks covered purposeful labels, concise instructions, natural wording and explicit recovery actions. Browser usability, legal review and production feature behaviour are not verified.
