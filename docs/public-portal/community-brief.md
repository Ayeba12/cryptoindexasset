# Homepage people sections

Scope: extend the approved public homepage with trader-profile and testimonial marquees, copy-guide actions and an honest service-principles section. Persuade mode. No dashboard, authentication, database or deployment changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: introduce the people-card layouts without presenting invented social proof as evidence.

OWN-WORLD: inherit Space Grotesk headings, Geist Mono text, zinc surfaces, yellow controls, shared radii and the established public spacing.

STORY: understand the service principles, compare example trader information, read illustrative visitor perspectives, then open the copy-trading guide.

FIRST VIEWPORT: preserve the homepage hero. The service section pairs a sticky heading with three open editorial rows. Each people section has a left-aligned H2, explicit demo context and stationary controls above a horizontal card row. Trader cards group portrait/name, style, copier count, accuracy, rating and a copy-guide action. Quotes lead testimonial cards; portraits close them.

FORM: narrow incumbent extension, no seed or new comp. Slow linear loops pause on hover, focus, hidden tab or offscreen. View-all and reduced-motion modes expose a static grid.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Content and motion boundary

All six personas and AI-generated portraits are fictional. Every card carries a visible demo/sample label. Accuracy is defined as an illustrative winning-closed-trades percentage over 90 days, not a forecast. Copier counts and ratings are mock values, not supplied performance records. Quotes describe hypothetical needs, not experienced financial outcomes. Copy trader buttons open the public guide; they do not start copying. No verification badges, live copy actions or structured-data reviews. Replace with approved records and consented testimonials before publication.

The service rows do not repeat unsupported promises of first-confirmation approval, guaranteed growth or absolute account security. They describe available 2FA, authenticated access, visible request status, network-dependent processing and copy-trading risk. Production behaviour remains a release dependency.

The motion purpose is occasional marketing browsing, not moving operational financial data. CSS transform loops use linear easing and a 72-second cycle. No per-frame JS, new animation dependency, animated figures or hover lifts. Static grids remain usable without JavaScript and under reduced motion. Primary card actions pause with keyboard focus; scrolling copies are hidden from assistive technology and inert.
