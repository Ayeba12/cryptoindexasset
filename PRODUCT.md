## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

The user describes a crypto copy-trading, investment and asset-management platform for retail investors and portfolio managers. Its three portal surfaces are the public website, customer dashboard and administrator control panel.

## Intended capabilities

The project brief includes multi-currency balances, trader profiles and copying, deposits, withdrawal requests, trading signals and administrator review. These are product requirements, not a statement that every flow is implemented or independently verified. The current evidence register is `docs/public-portal/evidence-and-decisions.md`.

## Constraints

- Preserve user records, balances, credentials, wallet mappings and transaction history.
- Keep financial operations and authorization server-controlled.
- Keep public design work separate from dashboard and administrator styling.
- Test locally before deployment. Design approval is not approval to publish a financial service.

## Brand commitments

Use the CA mark alone in public navigation and footer branding. Retain the typography in `DESIGN.md`. The user approved the Dovetail-led public preview's layout, spacing and restrained motion. Dashboard controls retain the shared application design system. Public imagery must match the selected theme.

## Evidence on hand

Public page drafts and sitemap are in `docs/public-portal/`. Approved theme-paired concept images and their provenance are documented in [the artwork README](output/public-portal-preview/theme-assets/README.md) and [asset manifest](output/public-portal-preview/theme-assets/manifest.json). These illustrations are not real account or verified trader data.

## Open decisions

Legal entity, eligibility, costs, custody, supported networks, settlement timing, support contact and verified execution behaviour remain release dependencies. Do not invent partners, ratings, returns, regulatory status or protections to fill these gaps.
